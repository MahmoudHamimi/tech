Next deadlines

    The date that costs you money is not the end date.

    Most contracts renew automatically unless you give notice weeks or months early. Tenor works out that last safe day for every agreement and puts it first.

    Worked example Ends 31 Dec − 60 days notice = act by 1 Nov
    Store

    One vault for every agreement

    Counterparty, category, annual value, start and end dates, owner, a link to the signed copy and notes. Search and filter across all of it.

    Track

    Notice deadlines, not just end dates

    A runway chart shows how long you have before each notice window opens. Renew in one click and the new end date and history are recorded.

    Alert

    Alerts at the lead times you choose

    Pick 90, 60, 30, 14 or 7 days. Alerts grow more urgent as the deadline closes and stay open until you mark them handled.

    Tenor runs entirely in your browser. Contracts are saved on this device, nothing is uploaded, and alerts appear in the vault each time you open it.
