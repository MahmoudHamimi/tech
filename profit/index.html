How the numbers are calculated

True net profit
Gross sales minus refunds, cost of goods, shipping, platform and payment fees, advertising and operating expenses.
Break-even ROAS
One divided by the contribution margin before ads. A channel below this return loses money on every order it drives.
Verified ROAS
Platform-reported revenue adjusted for duplicate attribution (sample assumption: Meta 72%, Google 82%, TikTok 64%), divided by spend.
Net contribution
Verified revenue times the contribution margin before ads, minus the channel's ad spend.
MER
Marketing efficiency ratio: gross sales divided by total ad spend across all channels.
Fees
Platform subscription and percentage fees plus card processing at 2.9% and 30 cents per order.

All figures are generated sample data for demonstration. Nothing here is connected to a live store.
